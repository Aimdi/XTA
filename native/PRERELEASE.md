**XTA Native is a prototype**: XTA written for Android alone (Kotlin and Jetpack Compose) instead of Flutter, to compare size, start time and feel with XTA itself.

- It installs **next to XTA** (app id `com.aimdi.xtanative`) and shares nothing with it: XTA, its data and its releases are untouched.
- To try it with your own setup, export your data in XTA (Settings → Data → Export, with "Export accounts?" switched on) and choose that file in XTA Native: your X accounts and the accounts you follow come with it.
- It is read-only towards X, like XTA.

This is not a release: features are missing on purpose, and it may be withdrawn.
